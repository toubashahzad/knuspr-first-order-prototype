# Progress to the minimum

**Functions:** `snack(id)` (toast), `goalHtml(t)` (drawer and cart), `fillHtml(t)`

- **Toast** after adding an item, at the bottom of the page: "Still €19.01 to the €20 minimum order. Reach it for free delivery and 50% off your first order, up to €20." with a pill progress bar.
- When the minimum is reached the toast turns green: "Minimum reached. Free delivery, and €10.57 off at checkout. Add €18.85 to get the full €20."
- **In the cart:** the same progress card above the items; hidden once the minimum is reached.
- **Maia top-up:** a line of text under the items, "Not sure what else to add? Let Maia top up your cart to €20". It adds basics until the minimum is reached.
