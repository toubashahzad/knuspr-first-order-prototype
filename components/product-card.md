# Product card

**Function:** `cardHtml(x)` with `picHtml`, `ribbonHtml`, `attrsHtml`, `chipsHtml`, `priceHtml`

- **Image** on white, clickable to open the product pop-up.
- **Corner ribbon** for price labels: Best price (blue), sale −20% (red), −42% Save the food (green).
- **Round attribute icons** on the photo for product labels: BIO, vegan, gluten-free.
- **Heart** top-right, translucent round button, no shadow.
- **Price**, old price struck through, name (clickable), weight and unit price ("750 g (1 kg = €5.08)"), info chips (Own brand, Local, deposit).
- **Action:** "+ Add" → the [stepper](stepper.md).
