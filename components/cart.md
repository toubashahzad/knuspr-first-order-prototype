# Cart drawer and full cart

**Functions:** `renderDrawer(t)`, `renderCart(t)`, `grouped(lines, row)`

- **Header:** cart icon with count, total, "Open full cart ›".
- **Items grouped by aisle** (Fruit & vegetables, Dairy, Frozen, …) with a light heading: name, thin line, count. One card, not split into boxes.
- **Footer:** total with "ca." when weighed items are in the cart, then "ca. because some items are priced by weight. You pay the exact amount after packing."
- **Button:** greyed out "Add €X more to check out" below the minimum, "Go to checkout" once reached.
- **Empty cart:** "Not sure where to start? Let Maia fill your cart to €20", then "Your cart is empty".
- No Xtra banner on a first order.
