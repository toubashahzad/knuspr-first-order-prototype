# Header and shopping row

**Function:** `renderHeader(t)` · **Markup:** `header.top`, `.shopbar`

Two rows. Only the shopping row is sticky on scroll.

- **Strip:** First delivery free · €20 minimum order · Up to €20 off your first order.
- **Row 1:** logo, delivery chip, Xtra banner, favourites heart, account.
  - Delivery chip states: *Enter your postcode* → *Berlin 12107 / Enter your exact address* → *Street / Your slot*. Opens the postcode check or the Delivery pop-up.
  - Favourites: round heart button with a count badge.
  - Account: "Sign in" for a guest, initials once details are saved at checkout.
- **Row 2 (sticky):** Categories, search (full width), cart icon with item count and amount underneath. The badge turns green when the minimum is reached.
- Ask Maia is a floating button in the bottom-right corner, not part of the header.
